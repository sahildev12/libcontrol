## Top 10 key selling points (ranked)

### 1. One system for daily library operations
Seats, students, enquiries, fees, and renewals in a single web app — no switching between register, Excel, and chat groups.

### 2. Visual seat map staff actually use
Colour-coded hall map. Assign, transfer, and release seats in clicks — not arguments at the front desk.

### 3. Built for Indian reading libraries
Half-day / full-day slots, custom library hours, ₹ fee tracking, monthly/yearly plans, and IST-friendly scheduling.

### 4. Trial-to-member workflow
Dedicated trial seat flow: temporary assignment, expiry visibility, optional trial fee, one-click convert to regular member.

### 5. Fee flexibility that matches real libraries
Monthly, yearly, one-time, and custom plans. Full payment or installments (weekly, monthly, quarterly, etc.). Partial payments and receive-payment tracking.

### 6. Two workspaces — owner vs desk staff
- **Platform Admin** — network view, branch comparison, revenue (Pro)  
- **Branch Panel** — daily operations without admin clutter  

### 7. Fast go-live (days, not months)
Five-step onboarding: branch → halls → seats → students → fees. No heavy IT project.

### 8. Smart hall & seat numbering
Create fresh halls starting at seat 1, or **optionally continue numbering** from a chosen hall (e.g. Hall 1 ends at 10 → Hall 2 starts at 11).

### 9. Student operations beyond a register
Auto student codes, ID cards (print/download), document storage, registration invite links, enquiry → student conversion.

### 10. Transparent SaaS pricing
Starter and Pro plans with clear seat/hall/branch limits. Yearly billing saves two months. Custom quote for large networks.

## Proof points (say with confidence)

- **96 automated tests** in the product codebase (quality baseline for enterprise buyers who ask about stability).
- **Activity log & audit trail** on Pro — who changed what, when.
- **Plan expiry reminder emails** to students (configurable days before expiry).

## What NOT to claim

| Do not say | Reality |
|------------|---------|
| "Expenses module is included" | Not shipped in app today |
| "Unlimited everything on Starter" | 100 seats / 5 halls / 1 branch |
| "Works offline" | Web-based; needs internet |
| "Mobile app in Play Store" | Responsive web; no native app yet |
| "Automatic WhatsApp fee reminders" | Email expiry reminders yes; WhatsApp is for sales/support contact |
