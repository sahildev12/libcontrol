# LibControl — Demo Walkthrough Script (30 minutes)

> **Goal:** Show the client's daily workflow, not every screen.  
> **Duration:** 25–30 min demo + 5 min Q&A  
> **Prep:** Use demo/staging environment with sample branch, 1–2 halls, mixed seat statuses.

---

## Before the call (5 min prep)

- [ ] Confirm: single branch or multi-branch prospect → pick Starter vs Pro storyline  
- [ ] Browser zoom 100%; close unrelated tabs  
- [ ] Demo login ready (branch user + optionally admin for Pro pitch)  
- [ ] Sample data: at least 1 vacant seat, 1 occupied, 1 trial, 1 expiring plan  
- [ ] Have pricing doc open (`04-pricing-and-plans.md`) for closing  

**Opening question:** *"Walk me through how a new student joins today — from enquiry to paying and getting a seat."*  
(Listen — mirror their words during demo.)

---

## Part 1 — Hook (2 min)

**Say:**  
*"LibControl replaces the register, the WhatsApp group, and the Excel sheet with one live system. I'll show you exactly what your desk staff would use tomorrow morning."*

**Show:** Branch dashboard  
- Point to seat KPIs (total, occupied, vacant, trial)  
- Today's overview — revenue, expiring plans  
- *"Before anyone sits down, you know today's money and who needs renewal."*

---

## Part 2 — Seat map (8 min) ⭐ Most important

**Navigate:** Seats → Seat Map  

**Say:**  
*"This is what ends the 'who is on seat 14?' argument."*

**Do:**
1. Show colour legend — vacant / occupied / trial / expiring  
2. Click a **vacant** seat → show assign flow  
3. Pick student, time slot (full day or custom hours), fee type  
4. Show seat turn **occupied**  
5. Click **trial seat** or open Trial Seats module briefly  
6. Mention **transfer** — move student without losing fee history  

**Pro tip:** If they care about halls, mention continue numbering when adding Hall 2.

---

## Part 3 — Students & enquiries (5 min)

**Students:**  
- Show student list, search, student code  
- Open one profile — photo, documents  
- **ID card** — print/download (always gets a reaction)  
- Registration invite link — *"Send link, student registers themselves"*

**Enquiries:**  
- Add quick enquiry → convert to student  
- *"Walk-in captured in 10 seconds, not on a loose paper."*

---

## Part 4 — Fees (7 min)

**Navigate:** Fees  

**Show:**
- List with payment status and plan status filters  
- One **expiring soon** row — tie to dashboard  
- **Receive payment** / partial payment on a due fee  
- Briefly mention installments for larger plans  

**Say:**  
*"Monthly, yearly, one-time, custom — however you sell memberships today, you can record it here."*

---

## Part 5 — Pro only: Owner view (5 min)

*Skip if Starter-only prospect.*

**Switch to Platform Admin dashboard**

**Show:**
- All branches table — occupancy %, revenue  
- Revenue chart  
- Switch into a branch — *"Owner can drill down without calling the manager"*  
- Mention activity log for accountability  

---

## Part 6 — Settings & onboarding (3 min)

**Show (quick):**
- Library hours — affects seat slot labels  
- Branding logo on login  
- Halls — create hall, capacity, optional continue numbering  

**Say:**  
*"Five steps: branch → halls → seats → students → fees. Most libraries go live in a few days."*

---

## Close (3 min)

**Recap their pain in their words + how LibControl fixed each.**

**Recommend plan:**
- 1 branch → **Starter** ₹299/mo or ₹2,990/yr  
- 2 branches → **Pro** ₹799/mo  
- 3+ → **Custom quote**

**CTA:**  
*"I can send a proposal today. When would you like to start onboarding — this week or next?"*

| Issue | Recovery |
|-------|----------|

| Slow load | Refresh; mention live polling every 15s |
