## Inside spread 4 — How it works

### Headline
Go live in days, not months

1. **Create branch & halls** — Add your centre, halls, and operating hours.  
2. **Set up seats** — Define seat numbers; continue numbering across halls if needed.  
3. **Assign seats** — Put students on regular or trial seats.  
4. **Add students** — Register members; capture walk-in enquiries.  
5. **Manage fees & renewals** — Collect payments and track plans from day one.
