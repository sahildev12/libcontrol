# LibSpace — Sales FAQ & Objection Handling

---

## General questions

### What is LibSpace?
LibSpace is web-based software for reading libraries and study centres. It manages seat maps, student records, trial seats, fees, renewals, and — on Pro — multiple branches from one owner dashboard.

### Who is it for?
Owners and managers of reading libraries, study halls, and co-working study spaces — especially those still using registers, Excel, or WhatsApp for operations.

### Do we need to install anything?
No. Staff use a web browser on computer or phone. You need internet.

### Is there a mobile app?
Not a native app. The interface is mobile-responsive in the browser.

### Is our data safe?
Data is stored on your server (or Phenomit-hosted cloud). Access is login-protected; branch users only see their branch. Pro includes activity logs for audit.

### Can we try before buying?
Yes — book a demo. Offer a guided walkthrough with their real workflow (seat map → assign → fee).

## Pricing objections

### "It's too expensive."
*Response:* Starter is ₹299/month — less than one student's monthly fee at most libraries. Ask: *"How much do you lose when one renewal is missed?"* Compare to cost of staff time on manual tracking.

### "We only have 30 seats — we don't need software."
*Response:* Small libraries benefit most from clarity — trial conversions and fee dues are harder to track manually at any size. Starter fits up to 100 seats.

### "Why yearly? We prefer monthly."
*Response:* Monthly is available. Yearly saves two months — recommend yearly once they are committed after 1–2 months on monthly.

### "Can we pay once and own it forever?"
*Response:* Current model is subscription (monthly/yearly) which includes updates and support. Custom/on-premise can be discussed for enterprise — route to Phenomit.

## Feature objections

### "We already use Excel."
*Response:* Excel does not show a **live seat map**, cannot block double-booking a seat, and does not send expiry reminders. LibSpace is built for real-time desk operations.

### "We use WhatsApp groups."
*Response:* WhatsApp is great for chat, terrible as a database. Who is on seat 23? Who paid partial fee last Tuesday? LibSpace answers in one click.

### "Our staff are not technical."
*Response:* Branch panel is designed for front desk — large seat map, few clicks to assign. We provide onboarding support (priority on Pro).

### "We need expenses / accounting."
*Response:* Be honest — **expense tracking is not in LibSpace today**. Fee collection and renewal tracking are. For full accounting they may keep Tally/Excel for books; LibSpace handles seat-student-fee operations.

### "We need WhatsApp reminders to students."
*Response:* LibSpace sends **email** plan expiry reminders when SMTP is set up. In-app notifications alert staff. WhatsApp integration is not live today.

## Technical objections

### "Can it run on our server?"
Custom plan — cloud or on-premise can be scoped. Standard deploy is PHP + MySQL on their hosting or Phenomit-managed server.

### "What if internet goes down?"
Operations pause until internet returns — same as any cloud tool. Recommend backup internet (mobile hotspot) for desk.

### "Can we migrate our existing student data?"
Discuss CSV/manual import scope with delivery team — not self-service in app today. Plan data migration in onboarding.

---

## Competitive objections

### "There is cheaper software."
*Response:* Ask what they include. Many tools are generic CRMs, not **seat-map-first** library systems. LibSpace is purpose-built for reading libraries with trials, slot hours, and Indian fee patterns.

### "We'll build our own."
*Response:* True cost of custom build + maintenance exceeds years of LibSpace subscription. Phenomit already built and tests 96+ automated scenarios.
