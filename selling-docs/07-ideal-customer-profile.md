# LibControl — Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

---

## Primary ICP — "Growing reading library owner"

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Business type** | Reading library, study hall, competitive exam centre, co-working study space |
| **Locations** | 1–2 branches today; plans to expand or already struggling with 2 |
| **Seats** | 20–300+ active seats |
| **Staff** | 2–15 (owner + front desk + 1 manager) |
| **Current tools** | Paper register, Excel, WhatsApp groups, maybe a generic billing app |
| **Revenue model** | Monthly/yearly student memberships; trials common |
| **Geography** | Tier 2–3 cities in India (Haryana, Rajasthan, UP, MP, etc.) — high density of study libraries |
| **Decision maker** | Owner or director (often also runs operations) |
| **Budget** | Can afford ₹299–799/month if ROI is clear |

---

## Strong fit signals (qualify IN)

- [ ] Arguments about seat allocation happen weekly  
- [ ] Trial students don't convert because nobody tracks expiry  
- [ ] Owner cannot answer "how much did we collect today?" without calling staff  
- [ ] Renewals are tracked manually; students leave before follow-up  
- [ ] Planning second branch within 12 months  
- [ ] Wants professional image (ID cards, digital records)  
- [ ] Staff turnover — new staff need a system, not tribal knowledge  

---

## Weak fit signals (qualify OUT or defer)

- [ ] Pure book lending library (no assigned seats)  
- [ ] Under 15 seats and no growth plan — may not justify even Starter  
- [ ] Needs full accounting / GST invoicing as primary product  
- [ ] Requires offline-only operation  
- [ ] Expects native mobile app as mandatory  
- [ ] Wants expenses module today — set expectation or wait for roadmap  
- [ ] No smartphone/computer at front desk  

---

## Personas

### Persona A — "The Owner-Operator"
- Runs desk and business  
- Pain: no time, everything in head  
- Sell: dashboard, renewals, revenue today  
- Plan: Starter → Pro when branch 2 opens  

### Persona B — "The Network Builder"
- Already 2 locations or franchise model  
- Pain: no central visibility  
- Sell: platform admin, branch comparison, activity log  
- Plan: Pro or Custom  

### Persona C — "The Desk Manager" (champion, not buyer)
- Front desk daily user  
- Pain: seat conflicts, angry students  
- Sell: seat map, quick assign, trial conversion  
- Bring owner to demo for pricing close
