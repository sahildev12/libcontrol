# LibControl — Pricing & Plans

> **Source:** Public pricing on LibControl landing page + `config/libcontrol.php` system limits.  
> **Note:** Prices exclude GST unless your quote says otherwise.

---

## Billing model

- **Subscription** — monthly or yearly (not one-time license on current public pricing)
- **Yearly discount** — pay yearly, save **2 months** vs paying monthly for 12 months
- **Same core features** on Starter and Pro — difference is **capacity** and **multi-branch admin**

---

## Public pricing (INR)

### Starter — Single branch setup

| Billing | List (strikethrough) | **Sell price** | Per-month equivalent |
|---------|----------------------|----------------|---------------------|
| Monthly | ₹499 | **₹299/mo** | ₹299 |
| Yearly | ₹5,990 | **₹2,990/yr** | ~₹249/mo |

**Limits:** 100 seats · 5 halls · 1 branch

**Best for:** One reading centre moving off registers and WhatsApp.

---

### Pro — Multi-branch setup *(Most popular)*

| Billing | List (strikethrough) | **Sell price** | Per-month equivalent |
|---------|----------------------|----------------|---------------------|
| Monthly | ₹1,199 | **₹799/mo** | ₹799 |
| Yearly | ₹11,990 | **₹7,990/yr** | ~₹666/mo |

**Limits:** 500 seats · 10 halls · 2 branches

**Best for:** Owner running 2 branches who needs central network view.

**Pro-only highlights:**
- Platform admin + branch panels
- Multi-branch analytics dashboard
- Activity log & audit trail
- Registration invites & QR-style links
- Priority onboarding support

---

### Custom — Tailored quote

**Limits:** Negotiated (seats, halls, 5+ branches typical)

**Includes everything in Pro, plus:**
- Custom seat / hall / branch limits
- Custom features or workflows (scoped separately)
- Large networks & franchise setups
- Cloud or on-premise deployment options
- Dedicated onboarding & training

**Sales rule:** Never quote a custom price without Phenomit approval.

## Upgrade paths (talk track)

| Situation | Recommend |
|-----------|-----------|
| 1 branch, &lt;100 seats | **Starter** monthly → yearly when committed |
| Opening 2nd branch | **Pro** |
| 3+ branches or franchise | **Custom** quote |
| Hitting seat cap on Starter | **Pro** or **Custom** |
| Needs audit log for compliance | **Pro** minimum |

## Sample ROI conversation

**Scenario:** 80-seat library, ₹1,500/month average fee, loses 3 renewals/month from poor tracking.

- Lost revenue: ~₹4,500/month  
- LibControl Starter: ₹299/month
