1. What This Page Covers

This page explains how to request deletion of your LibControl account and the personal information linked to it, including information we receive when you sign up or sign in with Facebook or Google. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Receive from Facebook or Google

When you choose to sign up or sign in with Facebook or Google, we receive only the basic profile information needed to create and identify your account:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • A unique account identifier issued by Facebook or Google.

We do not receive or store your Facebook or Google password, and we do not post anything to your Facebook or Google account.

3. How to Request Deletion

Send an email to support@libcontrol.in with the subject "Data deletion request". Please include:

  • The email address you used to sign up.
  • Your library's LibControl web address (for example, yourlibrary.libcontrol.in), if you created one.

Please send the request from the same email address used on your account so that we can confirm it belongs to you. We may ask for additional confirmation before deleting anything.

4. Removing LibControl from Your Facebook Account

You can also stop LibControl from receiving any further information from Facebook at any time:

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Open Apps and websites.
  3. Find LibControl and choose Remove.

Removing the app from Facebook stops future access but does not by itself delete the information already held by LibControl. To delete that information, please also send the email request described in Section 3.

5. What Happens After You Request Deletion

  • We will confirm receipt of your request and complete the deletion within 30 days.
  • Your account, sign-in details, and the information received from Facebook or Google will be deleted.
  • If you created a library on a free trial, that library and all of the records entered into it will also be deleted.
  • We will email you when the deletion is complete.

Free trial libraries that are not upgraded are deleted automatically 30 days after the trial ends, after warning emails are sent to the account owner.

6. Information We May Need to Keep

We may retain limited information where required by law, for example billing and tax records for paid subscriptions, or where needed to prevent fraud or abuse. Such information is kept only for as long as necessary and is then deleted or anonymised. Copies in routine backups are overwritten in the normal backup cycle.

7. Records Held by Your Library

If you are a student or member of a library that uses LibControl, your records are managed by that library. Please contact the library directly to request deletion. If you are unable to reach them, email support@libcontrol.in and we will help direct your request.
