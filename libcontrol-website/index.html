What LibControl fixes

From daily chaos to one live system

Every problem below maps to something LibControl handles out of the box — seats, students, fees, trials, and branches in one place.

01

Manual registers everywhere

Seat logs, fee notes, and student details spread across notebooks and loose sheets.

LibControl fix

One student registry with seat history, documents, and fee records linked to each member.

02

Seat allocation confusion

Staff disagree on who sits where — regular, trial, and vacant seats get mixed up.

LibControl fix

Live seat map with colour-coded occupied, vacant, trial, and expiring states per hall.

03

Missed renewals

Plans expire quietly. Members leave before anyone notices the due date passed.

LibControl fix

Expiry alerts, renewal tracking, and dashboard flags for plans ending in the next 7 days.

04

Fee tracking falls apart

Partial payments, dues, and instalments tracked in memory or on WhatsApp chats.

LibControl fix

Fee plans with partial payments, instalments, and full payment history on every booking.

05

Trial seats hard to manage

Trial periods, free hours, and conversions tracked with no clear view on the map.

LibControl fix

Dedicated trial seat module — duration, optional fee, and convert-to-member from the map.

06

No view across branches

Spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups can’t show occupancy, fees, or performance network-wide.

LibControl fix

Platform admin dashboard — branch comparison, revenue, occupancy, and attention items in one view.
