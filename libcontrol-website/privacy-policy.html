1. Introduction

Phenomit (“Phenomit”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) operates LibControl, a software solution designed to support library management, study-seat management, administrative operations, and related activities.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, store, disclose, and protect information when you visit https://libcontrol.in, submit an enquiry or demo request, register your interest in our services, communicate with our team, or access and use LibControl through your library or organisation.

This policy applies to website visitors, prospective customers, authorised library representatives, software users, and other individuals whose personal information we process in connection with our website and services.

By accessing our website or using our services, you acknowledge that you have had an opportunity to review this Privacy Policy. Where consent or another lawful basis is required for processing, we will rely on the applicable lawful basis in accordance with relevant data protection laws.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that is reasonably necessary to operate our website, respond to enquiries, provide LibControl, support our customers, and fulfil applicable legal obligations.

2.1 Contact and Enquiry Information

When you request a product demonstration, submit a pre-registration form, contact our team, or enquire about LibControl, we may collect:

  • Name of the individual submitting the enquiry.
  • Mobile number and email address.
  • Library, institution, or organisation name.
  • City, state, and other relevant business location details.
  • Information about your library's requirements, expected usage, or requested services.
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide through a form, email, telephone call, or other communication channel.

We use this information primarily to respond to your enquiry, arrange demonstrations, explain product features, and facilitate potential onboarding.

2.2 Account and Authentication Information

When a library or organisation uses LibControl, we may process information required to create, authenticate, and administer user accounts, including:

  • Usernames, login identifiers, and account details.
  • Passwords stored or processed using appropriate security mechanisms.
  • User roles, access permissions, and account status.
  • Branch or library identifiers associated with an account.
  • Account activity and relevant authentication logs.

Users should not share their passwords or authentication credentials with unauthorised individuals. Access should be granted only to staff members who require it for their responsibilities.

2.3 Library and Operational Information

Depending on the features used by a subscribing library, LibControl may process operational information entered, uploaded, or maintained by the library and its authorised staff.

This may include:

  • Library membership and registration records.
  • Student or member names and contact information.
  • Study-seat allocation, booking, occupancy, and usage records.
  • Library branch, seat, and facility information.
  • Subscription, payment, billing, or fee records, where applicable.
  • Administrative records, reports, and other information entered into the software.
  • Communications or notes maintained within the system, where supported.

The nature of the information collected depends on the library's configuration and its use of the software.

Libraries and other organisations using LibControl are responsible for determining what information they enter into the system and ensuring that they have the appropriate authority and lawful basis to collect and process that information.

2.4 Technical and Usage Information

When you visit our website or access our services, certain technical information may be collected automatically, where enabled and permitted by applicable law.

This may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device type, operating system, and technical identifiers.
  • Website pages visited and relevant interaction data.
  • Date, time, and duration of access.
  • Error reports, diagnostic information, and security logs.
  • Referring website or source, where available.

We use this information to maintain website functionality, troubleshoot technical problems, monitor performance, protect against unauthorised access, and improve the user experience.

2.5 Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential functionality, maintain security, remember preferences, or understand website performance.

Where applicable, non-essential cookies or tracking technologies will be used in accordance with the choices available to users and relevant legal requirements.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality or availability of some website features.

Analytics and advertising tools (Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel) are used only if you accept them in our cookie banner. See Section 12 for details.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, provide our services, meet contractual commitments, comply with applicable legal requirements, and resolve disputes.

Retention periods may vary depending on the category of information and the circumstances in which it was collected.

For example:

  • Enquiry information: Retained for the period necessary to respond to requests, manage prospective customer relationships, and maintain appropriate business records.
  • Account information: Retained while an account remains active and for a reasonable period afterwards where necessary for contractual, legal, security, or dispute-resolution purposes.
  • Library operational records: Retained in accordance with the applicable service arrangement, the library's instructions, and relevant retention requirements.
  • Billing and accounting records: Retained for the periods required by applicable financial, tax, and accounting laws.
  • Security and technical logs: Retained for an appropriate period based on security, operational, and legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it, anonymise it, or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal obligations, backup processes, and legitimate retention requirements.

Customers should contact Phenomit before account termination if they require assistance with data export, retention, or deletion, and should maintain their own appropriate backups of important records.

Phenomit takes reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, accidental loss, alteration, disclosure, destruction, or misuse.

Depending on the service and technical environment, these measures may include access restrictions, authentication controls, secure data-handling practices, system monitoring, and appropriate maintenance procedures.

However, no website, software platform, network, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security against every possible threat or unauthorised activity.

Users and customer organisations also play an important role in maintaining security. We recommend that they:

  • Use strong, unique passwords.
  • Avoid sharing account credentials.
  • Assign permissions according to staff responsibilities.
  • Remove access promptly when a staff member no longer requires it.
  • Keep devices and browsers updated.
  • Notify us promptly of suspected unauthorised access or security incidents.

If a personal data breach occurs, Phenomit will take appropriate steps to investigate, contain, and address the incident and make any notifications required by applicable law.
